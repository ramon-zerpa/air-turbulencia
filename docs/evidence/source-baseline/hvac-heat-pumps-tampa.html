Your heat pump needs service if

Not cooling or heating

If your heat pump can't keep your home at the temperature you set, the system needs a diagnostic before the issue gets worse.

Not turning on

A heat pump that won't start at all almost always points to a control, capacitor, or compressor issue that needs a qualified technician.

Strange noises

Grinding, hissing, or banging from the outdoor unit is rarely cosmetic — it's a sign a component is failing and should be checked promptly.

Maintenance

Repair or replace?

Age

Heat pumps run efficiently for 10-15 years. Past that, even a clean repair is usually a short-term fix.

Frequent repairs

If you've called for service more than once in a year, the math typically favors a new system over chasing the next failure.

Loud, unusual noises

Persistent noise on an older unit usually means a major component is at end of life. We'll tell you what we'd do if it were our home.
